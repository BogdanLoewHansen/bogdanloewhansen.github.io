I am a researcher at the [Department of Engineering Cybernetics](https://www.ntnu.edu/itk) at NTNU, working with the [NTNU UAV Icing Lab](https://uavicinglab.com/). My work is on how atmospheric icing degrades the flight performance of small fixed-wing UAVs, and on how that degradation can be modelled, detected, and compensated for in flight.

I completed my PhD at NTNU in 2026 with the thesis [*Modeling and Validation of Atmospheric Icing Effects on Small Fixed-Wing UAVs*](https://hdl.handle.net/11250/5533084), following an MSc in Cybernetics and Robotics from the same department.

Research interests
======
My work concerns the operation of small fixed-wing UAVs and light aircraft in icing conditions, in particular:

- Modeling and identification of the effect of icing on propeller-based propulsion systems.
- System identification of aerodynamic models in nominal and iced conditions.
- Development of model-based change and fault detection algorithms.

Main project
======
[**Zero Ice Shield**](https://uavicinglab.com/2024/04/15/exploring-new-frontiers-icing-research-for-green-aviation/) — an ice protection solution for green aviation, developed in collaboration with [Ubiq Aerospace](https://www.ubiqaerospace.com/).

Projects for students, 2026/2027
======
Four projects are available for the coming academic year, spanning operational analysis, ice detection, and flight control:

1. [**Ice Protection or Weather Avoidance?**](/student-projects/#project-1) — quantify when an active ice protection system becomes commercially justified for Nordic cargo drone delivery.
2. [**The Aircraft as Its Own Virtual Ice Sensor**](/student-projects/#project-2) — indirect ice detection from flight performance alone, reporting severity rather than a binary alarm.
3. [**Qualifying a Virtual Ice Sensor**](/student-projects/#project-3) — benchmark detection strategies for propeller icing and establish how much model fidelity each demands.
4. [**Autopilot Tuning That Survives Icing**](/student-projects/#project-4) — re-anchor flight controllers onto flight-validated iced aerodynamics and tune across both envelopes.

Full descriptions, background requirements and references are on the [Student Projects](/student-projects/) page.
